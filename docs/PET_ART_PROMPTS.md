BOTTOM RIGHT: a lilac baby owl with a round fluffy body, short wings, and a tiny crescent-shaped natural feather marking.

Only FOUR characters, no extras, no text, labels, logos, borders, cards or checkerboard. The characters will be extracted into alpha-transparent game sprites; light cream fur must still have subtly defined coloured edges. No recognisable third-party characters.
